1. Executive Summary

The ABB Adaptive SOC Engine & Micro-Segmentation Platform is a specialized, real-time industrial cybersecurity operations center engineered for mission-critical Operational Technology (OT) and SCADA networks. Traditional IT security solutions fail in industrial environments because they prioritize confidentiality over operational availability, often shutting down entire manufacturing cells upon detecting anomalies. This platform bridges that gap by combining sub-second Deep Packet Inspection (DPI), hardware-enforced micro-segmentation, and an autonomous 7-step closed-loop remediation pipeline that isolates threats, dynamic-rotates compromised IP leases, and re-establishes authenticated TLS 1.3 tunnels to the Primary Core Server—achieving threat neutralization with zero aggregate production downtime.

2. Technical Capabilities & Sequence

Real-Time DPI Stream

Continuous ingestion of Modbus/TCP, IEC 60870-5-104, OPC UA, and DNP3. Catches forced register writes and out-of-band setpoints.

Purdue Model Micro-Segmentation

Bidirectional colored conduits (Downstream Lilac `#615EEF` and Upstream Violet `#9061F9`) separating Robotics, Process Chem, and Substation RTUs.

Autonomous 7-Step Response Protocol:
• Step 01 (Detection - 3s): Anomaly flagged by DPI signature matching.
• Step 02 (Classification - 3s): CVE identification (e.g., CVE-2022-30777, CVE-2023-38035).
• Step 03 (Subnet ID - 3s): Target CIDR isolated from lateral pivot paths.
• Step 04 (Risk Eval - 3s): Real-time blast radius and physical asset risk computation.
• Step 05 (Isolation - 3s): Dynamic airgap quarantine applied to the affected node.
• Step 06 (Audit Log - 3s): Immutable RFC-5424/CEF audit record committed to SIEM storage.
• Step 07 (Remediation - 5s): Dynamic DHCP IP lease rotation (10.10.30.42 → 10.10.30.198), local ARP cache purge, and mutual TLS 1.3 session re-establishment with Main Server 10.10.0.1.

3. Strategic Value to ABB

• Protecting Human Life & Physical Machinery: Stops OT exploits before they cause turbine overspeeds, boiler ruptures, or robotic arm collisions.
• Eliminating Production Downtime: Instead of shutting down an entire factory floor or substation, it isolates only the single compromised node, rotates its IP lease, and restores communication in 5 seconds.
• Turnkey Global Compliance: Native adherence to IEC 62443-3-3, NIST SP 800-82 Rev 3, NERC CIP, and European NIS2 directives.
• Commercialization in ABB Ability™: Can be delivered as an integrated cyber-resilience module across ABB drives, PLCs, RTUs, and robotics.
